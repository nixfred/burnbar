🔥📊 Burn Bar 2.0 for Omarchy

I pay for Claude, Codex, Grok and Kimi. The bar already told me how much I burned on each one. It never told me which one to use right now. So I made it tell me.

Mine says USE GROK NEXT. I am 3% into Grok's week and the week is half over, so 49% of it is sitting in the bank. Claude says COME BACK IN 8:36:42, because I burned 10% of the week in the first 5% of it. Codex is spent until Saturday afternoon. Kimi is on pace.

The banked number is a clock. Stay off a sub and watch it climb. Get behind and you get a countdown to the moment you are even again.

One sub and it stays quiet, there is nothing to pick. It never points you at your local GPU. It will not send you to a sub whose 5 hour window is about to fill.

The rest of 2.0:

One big card per sub with everything about it.
A burndown graph on every card.
Cards glow, and you pick what the glow means.
A SETUP page with every option on one screen.
Nothing scrolls and it fits a 1080p laptop.

Before I shipped it I handed about 7,800 lines to Grok 4.6 and Kimi k3 and told them to find what I got wrong. 22 real bugs. The worst one: with Kimi and the GPU lane both turned on, the GPU lane was drawing right on top of Kimi. On my own machine. I had not noticed. The whole list is in the repo, including the two findings I threw out and why.

Today: 16.1M tokens in 24 hours, 2,404 turns, 75 sessions.
